Preservation Notes

This is a clean-room, browser-native recreation built from static inspection of the preserved Wargame 1.5.1 IPA. The original ARM executable is not included in or executed by this web app.

The six faction records, all 91 country records, world geometry, adjacency, starting forces, unit balance, upgrade costs, localization, and artwork were recovered from the archived resources. Apple-specific PNGs were normalized into standard browser PNGs while the source files remain untouched in the preservation folder.

Original credits: Design & programming — Jérémie Di Prizio and Stéphane Di Prizio. General design & gameplay — Mathieu Di Prizio. Additional artwork & game design — Benoît Leygue.

This preservation edition stores campaigns only in your browser. It makes no network requests during play.
